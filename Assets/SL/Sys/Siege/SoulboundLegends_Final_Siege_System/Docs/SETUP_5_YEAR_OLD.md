# SIMPLE SETUP
1. Open your SL Unity project.
2. Unzip the ZIP.
3. Copy Runtime and Editor into Assets/SL/Siege.
4. Let Unity compile.
5. Create an empty GameObject named Siege System Manager.
6. Add SiegeWorldGenerator, SiegeMatchManager, SiegeControlCoreSystem, SiegeDestructionSystem, SiegeIntegrationBridge and SiegeGameBootstrap.
7. On SiegeWorldGenerator press AUTO-CREATE ALL CONNECTION SLOTS.
8. Connect the existing Terrain, Infrastructure, Destruction, Character, Combat, Skills, Equipment, Inventory, Stats/Battle Power, Server, Networking, Matchmaking, Team Balancing, General HUD, Rewards, Rank Advancement and Lobby systems.
9. Press VALIDATE SIEGE CONNECTIONS.
10. Fix every missing required connection.
11. Press GENERATE FINAL SIEGE WORLD.
12. Test Round 1, Round 2 and the 1-1 Final Siege on the server.
13. Do not add CS capture/first-to-five rules or BR safe-zone rules to Siege.
