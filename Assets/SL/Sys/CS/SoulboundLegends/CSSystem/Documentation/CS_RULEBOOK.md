# CS RULEBOOK — SOULBOUND LEGENDS

1. CS is a competitive team elimination mode.
2. Recommended format: 5 vs 5.
3. First team to 5 round victories wins the match.
4. Maximum 9 rounds.
5. Every round begins with all connected players alive.
6. Death eliminates a player for the current round.
7. No mid-round respawn.
8. Eliminated players may spectate eligible teammates.
9. A round is won when every opposing player has been eliminated.
10. There are no capture objectives in CS.
11. There are no Siege objectives in CS.
12. The seven sites within each map are combat arenas/locations, not objectives.
13. Player equipment used in CS is the equipment legitimately obtained in Adventure World.
14. Player power/stats/skills used in CS come from Adventure World progression.
15. Equipment is not permanently lost because of a CS death.
16. Matchmaking should consider player level, equipment strength, skills and overall battle power.
17. Team balancing should attempt to keep total combat power reasonably close.
18. Server authority must validate loadout, combat actions, damage, death, score and match result.
19. AFK, disconnect and abandonment handling is required.
20. Final rewards must be server-authorized and then passed to the shared progression system.
